Survival Escape

A simple 2D top-down survival game where the player must navigate through a dangerous area, avoid enemies, collect power-ups, and reach the blue exit to survive.

 How to Play

Goal:

Survive long enough to reach the blue exit.

Use the controls below to move around the map and avoid the enemies.

PlayerControls:

 Key - Action 
 W or ↑ - Move Up 
 A or ← - Move Left 
 S or ↓ - Move Down 
 D or → - Move Right 

❤️ Health

The player starts with 3 hearts:

- Each time an enemy touches the player, 1 heart is lost.
- The player is knocked backward when hit.
- Lost hearts turn gray.
- When all hearts are lost, the player "dies" and respawns at the green starting area.
- Respawning restores the player's health.

🔺 Enemies

Enemies wander around their assigned areas.

- Enemies have their own territory.
- They randomly wander around their starting area.
- If the player gets close enough, the enemy will chase them.
- Enemies will stop chasing once the player leaves their territory.
- Touching an enemy causes damage and knockback.

🟡 Power-Ups

Yellow power-ups are scattered throughout the map.


🟢 Start Area

The player begins at the green slime blob.

If the player loses all of their health, they respawn here.

🔵 Exit

The blue door is the player's escape point.

Reach the blue exit to win the game.

The exit has a subtle pulsing effect to make it easier to identify.
